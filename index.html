About the work

Aya paints in oil on canvas and in soft pastel on paper. Her subjects are close to home: a girl holding flowers, a bowl of strawberries, a bicycle parked outside a shop, a boat waiting for the night.

Color leads each piece. Warm skies, deep greens and strong pinks carry the mood, and the thick, textured paint in the flower fields and petals is left visible on purpose.

Every canvas carries this signature in the corner.
